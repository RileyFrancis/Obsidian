Below are the basic stats used by all survivors and monsters:
### Health
The health of a survivor or monster is how much damage that it can take before it dies. If a survivor or monster has a health total of 0 or lower, it dies.
### Sanity (survivor only)
Sanity represents the state of mind of the survivor. The lower the sanity, the more likely monsters will appear.
### Speed
Speed determines who hits first, the order of turns for monsters, and makes you harder to hit. When engaging in combat, both the attacker and defender add their speed scores to their attack roles. If the attacker's value is higher, they land a hit.
### Defense
Defense reduces the amount of damage that you take from physical attacks. 
### Attack (monster only)
Monsters have an attack stat that states how much damage they will deal to players when they deal combat damage. Attack values also have a damage type which can be either physical damage which is reduced by defense, or magic damage.
