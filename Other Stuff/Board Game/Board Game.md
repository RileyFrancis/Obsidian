### Lamplighter
The lamplighter is designed around controlling regions of the map by gathering information about areas.

The lamplighter begins the game with 8 flame tokens. These flame tokens can be placed on locations. When you place a flame token, immediately reveal all neighboring unexplored tiles (you do not get placement bonuses for these tiles). Locations with flame tokens and all neighboring locations can not be enshrouded.

## Flow of Play
### What Consists of a Round
A complete round is each of the players taking their turn plus the world action (described below). The world action takes place before the starting player's turn at every round except the first round.
### A Standard Turn
On a player's turn, they will do the following:
1. Draw three cards from their personal deck, plus any extra from card effects
2. Gain five energy tokens and place them in the reserve in your character board
3. Take any number of actions by playing cards, moving your character, taking actions on cards, or engaging in combat. For each of these actions, you must spend an amount of energy tokens required by the action.
4. Discard down to 7 cards and remove energy tokens in excess of 10. For every three energy tokens removed in this way, regain 1 health.
### World Action
1. Progress the time tracker one hour.
2. Each monster will take a turn, following their own set of rules or actions. The order of turns that monsters take is determined by their speed, and in case of a tie, is determined by the players.
3. All instances of "At the end of the round" abilities will trigger in an order determined by the players.
