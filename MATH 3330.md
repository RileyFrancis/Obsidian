> [!problem] Question 1
> Show that if $h, h': X \to Y$ are homotopic and $k,k':Y \to Z$ are homotopic then $h \circ k$ and $k' \circ h'$ are homotopic.

***Proof:*** Let $h,h':X\rightarrow Y$ be homotopic and $k,k':Y \to Z$ be homotopic. Since each $h,h',k,k'$ are homotopic, they are each continuous, so their compositions $h \circ k$ and $k' \circ h'$ are continuous.

> [!problem] Question 3

> 1. Find a star convex set that is not convex.
> 2. Show that if $A$ is star convex, $A$ is simply connected.
