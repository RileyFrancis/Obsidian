> [!problem] Question 1
> Let $X=\{a,b,c\}$. How many topologies are on $X$.

The following are unique topologies on $X$:
- $\{\emptyset, X\}$
- $\{\emptyset, \{a\}, X\}$
- $\{\emptyset, \{b\}, X\}$
- $\{\emptyset, \{c\}, X\}$
- $\{\emptyset, \{a,b\}, X\}$
- $\{\emptyset, \{a,c\}, X\}$
- $\{\emptyset, \{b,c\}, X\}$
- $\{\emptyset, \{a\}, \{a,b\}, X\}$
-  $\{\emptyset, \{a\}, \{a,b\}, X\}$
- $\{\emptyset, \{c\}, \{a,b\}, X\}$
- $\{\emptyset, \{b\}, \{a,c\}, X\}$
- $\{\emptyset, \{a\}, \{b,c\}, X\}$
- $\{\emptyset, \{a\}, \{b\}, \{a,b\}, X\}$
- $\{\emptyset, \{a\}, \{c\}, \{a,c\}, X\}$
- $\{\emptyset, \{b\}, \{c\}, \{b,c\}, X\}$
- $\{\emptyset, \{a\}, \{b\}, \{c\}, \{a,b\}, \{a,c\}, \{b,c\}, X\}$

There are a total of 29 unique topologies on $X$.
