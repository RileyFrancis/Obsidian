> [!tip] Siefert-van Kampen Theorem 
> Suppose $X$ is a space which is the union of closed subspaces $Y$ and $Z$ and there is a point $p$ such that $Y\cap Z=\{\}$. Then we call $X$ the wedge of $Y$ and $Z$ and write $X=Y\vee Z$.
